# FormDitto Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 October 2026

FormDitto is a browser extension that fills repetitive web forms from templates you save. **Your data never leaves your device.**

## What FormDitto stores

- **Templates:** the site address (origin) where you recorded the template and a description of each form field you selected: its label, name, type and position on the page.
- **Variants:** the values you entered or captured for those fields — this can be personal data such as names, e-mail addresses, phone numbers or postal addresses.
- **Settings:** interface language, theme, how the panel and the page sync while you record a template, and how long to wait for slow fields.

All of this is kept in your browser's local extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`) on this device. It is not synced to your browser account.

## What FormDitto does not do

- It has no server, no account and no sign-in.
- It does not collect analytics or telemetry and makes no network requests.
- It does not sell, share or transfer any data to anyone.
- It does not read or fill password fields, payment card fields or hidden fields.
- It does not submit forms.
- It does not access any website until you click its icon on that page, or allow it for a specific site.

## Limited Use

FormDitto's use of the data it handles adheres to the [Chrome Web Store User Data Policy](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/), including the Limited Use requirements. The data is used only to provide FormDitto's single purpose — filling the forms you choose — and never for advertising, credit or lending decisions, or any other purpose.

## Your control

- Export your templates to a JSON file or delete them at any time in the side panel.
- Remove site access in FormDitto settings or in your browser's extension settings.
- Uninstalling FormDitto deletes all of its stored data.

Exported JSON files are not encrypted. Store them like any other work document.

## Changes

If a future version offers an optional paid licence, activating it would contact the payment provider only to check the licence key, and only when you choose to activate. This policy will be updated before such a version is released.
